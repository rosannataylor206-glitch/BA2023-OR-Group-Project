## Main text · Algorithm 1. Evaluate integrated logistics policies

以下英文伪代码按 `joint_v9.py` 的 `build_policy()`、`run()` 和 `select_and_frontiers()` 整理。

```text
For each mission case and each operating mode (ideal or fault):
    Generate the construction splits and one-year water designs.
    For each elevator share s of the 100 million tonnes:
        Evaluate the construction-only baseline at share s.
        For each certified water design, preparation share and packing rule:
            Start a new ledger and schedule general construction at share s.
            Schedule processors, tanks and power equipment in separate batches.
            Deliver initial usable water and stage apex stocks when required.
            Record the upper bound on time to habitation.
            Account for the 365-day water service in the resource ledger.
            Evaluate the scenario budget and operational emissions proxy.
For the default mission:
    Compare construction baselines by route and operating mode.
    Under faults, compare budget with time to habitation and emissions.
    For each emissions cap, select the lowest-budget eligible candidate.
```

The construction share allocates general materials between the elevator chain and ground-launched rockets. The preparation share allocates late equipment and initial-water deliveries. The water design separately specifies the operating replenishment route, including the all-stock option with no scheduled replenishment.

For the environmental analysis, the selection rule is \(\min_{i\in\mathcal{P}} C_i\) subject to \(E_i\leq\epsilon\), where \(\mathcal{P}\) is the finite catalogue for the default mission and fault scenario, \(C_i\) is the scenario budget, and \(E_i\) is the modeled operational emissions proxy. The result is conditional on the chosen coefficient scenario and catalogue.

## Appendix A.1 · Construction delivery certificate under faults

实际摘自 `construction_v9.py` 中 `_route_plan()`，从第 211 行开始，调整了空格与换行。函数在这段之前已处理零需求和非正服务斜率。

```python
per_target = target / n
H = max(1, math.ceil((per_target + intercept) / slope))
spare = math.ceil(h * H / P + h - 1e-12) if h else 0
fleet_new = base_deficit + spare
fleet_present = max(base, available) + spare
deployment = (
    math.ceil(s['Tlift_days'] + x.climber_transit_days
              + dry_mass * fleet_new / lift_rate)
    if needs_deployment else 0
)
source_days = math.floor(H - deployment - Tpath)
source_mass = rate * source_days
attempts = math.ceil(source_mass / Q - 1e-12)
exact_loss = int(budget(H, j, P))
exact_hull_loss = int(budget(H, h, P))
settled_attempts_mass_lower = max(0., source_mass - 4 * Q)
guar_per = settled_attempts_mass_lower - Q * exact_loss
affine_lower = slope * H - intercept
if (source_days < 0 or guar_per < per_target - 1e-6
        or guar_per < affine_lower - 1e-6 or spare < exact_hull_loss
        or attempts * Q < source_mass - 1e-7
        or attempts * Q - source_mass > Q + 1e-7):
    raise AssertionError(
        'Construction certificate failed independent arithmetic guards'
    )
```

Here \(H\) is the delivery-time upper bound in days, \(n\) is the number of parallel sources, and \(Q\) is payload per attempt in tonnes. The code checks the guaranteed delivery per source after deducting bounded cargo losses and checks that the spare fleet covers bounded hull losses. The parameter \(P\) is the rolling budget period, while \(j\) and \(h\) are the per-source cargo and hull loss allowances for that period.

## Appendix A.2 · Certified reserve for a recycled water design

实际摘自 `water_v9.py` 中 `_component()`，从第 143 行开始，调整了空格与换行。`certificate()` 的具体区间计算可查 v9 调用的 `reference/water_v8.py` 第 36 行。

```python
cert = certificate(
    G, eta, Q, delta, rx['Lmax'], plant['K_t_day'],
    P=P, B=closed, R0=b['R0'], Rc=b['Rc'], Rp=b['Rp'], J=cargo_budget,
    tank_events=b['tank'], C=x.tank_capacity_t,
)
if not cert['certified']:
    return dict(
        feasible=False, route=route, G_t_day=G, eta=eta,
        packed_batch_t=packed, delta=delta, closed_budget=closed,
        receiver=rx, certificate=cert, reason=cert['reason'],
    )
reserve = math.ceil((cert['reserve_t'] + .25 * G) / 500) * 500
fresh_capacity = reserve + G
raw_capacity = G * (Rproc + 1)
fresh_cells = math.ceil(fresh_capacity / x.tank_capacity_t) + b['tank']
raw_cells = math.ceil(raw_capacity / x.tank_capacity_t) + b['tank']
```

Here \(G\) is daily service-water demand in tonnes per day, \(\eta\) is the recovery fraction, and \(Q\) is usable water per batch in tonnes. The certificate evaluates \(R_{\mathrm{cert}}=Q+\max_{n\in\mathcal I}[A(n)-S(n-E)]\), where \(A\) bounds accumulated demand, backlog and losses, \(S\) bounds guaranteed replenishment from below, \(E\) is the effective lead time, and \(\mathcal I\) is the finite interval scan. The implementation adds one-quarter day of demand and rounds the reserve upward in 500-tonne increments before sizing separate clean- and raw-water compartments. The all-stock alternative is evaluated separately using the 365-day demand and the tank-loss allowance.
